# Methodology

This directory presents the publicly available description of the agent-based methodology for the detection, attribution, and controlled de-anonymization of cyber threat actors in dynamic OSINT collections.

At the current stage, the directory contains only an extended methodological abstract and a concise presentation of the principal research concepts. The complete framework and the associated research paper will be added after their official publication.

## Methodological Scope

The methodology covers:

- transformation of a primary analytical prompt into a structured AgentFlow workflow;
- adaptive formation of a virtual expert swarm;
- construction of role-specific RAG contexts;
- repeated execution and iterative refinement of agent prompts;
- semantic canonicalization of multilingual entity mentions;
- canonicalization of attribution statements;
- separation of independent evidence from repeated publications;
- linguo-statistical aggregation of analytical results;
- attribution control and preservation of epistemic status;
- controlled de-anonymization based on evidential support;
- construction of a dynamic semantic knowledge graph;
- generation of explainable analytical reports.
